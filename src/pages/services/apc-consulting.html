Problems we solve

Sound familiar?

Disagreement

"Two labs gave us two different codes."

We compare calibration status, sample handling, dilution and reporting conventions to find where the results diverge, and which one to trust.

Instability

"Repeat runs on the same bottle don't match."

Air entrainment, poor agitation, coincidence, flow-rate problems or a failing sensor. We work through the candidates in order of likelihood.

Method

"We need a written particle counting procedure."

Sample preparation, degassing, dilution, acceptance criteria and reporting. Built around the standards your customers reference.

Transition

"Our spec still says NAS 1638."

We help you move to ISO 4406 or SAE AS4059 reporting and explain to customers what changes and what doesn't.

Audit

"An auditor is reviewing our particle counting."

We review your calibration records, procedures and reports against what an auditor will look for, before they do.

Targets

"We don't know what code to run at."

We help set realistic cleanliness targets from your components, operating pressure and the manufacturer's guidance.

How an engagement works

Scoped up front. Written down at the end.

  1. Describe the problem

    What you're seeing, what you expected, and any reports or raw data you can share.

  2. We scope it

    A short call or email exchange, then a written scope and fixed price or estimate.

  3. Investigation

    Data and procedure review, and test samples or instrument checks where they're needed.

  4. Findings

    A written summary of what we found, what it means, and exactly what to change.

Get a faster answer

What to send with your inquiry

The more we can see, the quicker we can tell you whether we can help.

  • Particle counter make, model and last calibration report
  • Raw counts per size channel, not only the final code
  • Any conflicting reports from other labs or instruments
  • Your sampling and analysis procedure, if one exists
  • The specification or customer requirement you report against
