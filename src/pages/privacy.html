Legal

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

What we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect the information you enter: your name, work email, company, and optionally your phone number, plus details about your instrument, samples or technical question. We don't ask for, and you shouldn't send, payment details through this form.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote and carry out any work you ask us to do. We don't add you to marketing lists without your separate consent, and we don't sell or rent your information.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered through our website hosting and form-handling provider, which processes the data on our behalf. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Analytics

If we use website analytics, we use them to understand how visitors find and use the site in aggregate, for example which pages are read and whether the inquiry form is working. Analytics are not used to identify you personally.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to respond and to maintain records of any work performed, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by contacting us.
