Three terms, often confused

Calibration, traceability and accreditation are not the same thing

Understanding the difference helps you decide what your quality system actually requires.

Calibration

Comparing an instrument against a known reference and documenting the relationship. For particle counters, ISO 11171 defines how that's done.

Traceability

An unbroken, documented chain of comparisons linking a result to a recognized reference. For ISO 11171 particle sizing, that reference is NIST SRM 2806.

Accreditation

Independent confirmation by an accreditation body that a laboratory is competent to perform specific calibrations or tests. Calibration and testing labs are typically accredited to ISO/IEC 17025.
