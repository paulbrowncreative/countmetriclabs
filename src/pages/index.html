FAQ

Common questions

Something else? Ask a technician.

Is CountMetric Labs accredited?

Not yet. We're in our development phase and working toward accreditation. Our reports state the method used and the traceability of our reference materials, and they don't carry any accreditation mark. Read our full status.

Which particle counters can you calibrate?

ISO 11171 covers automatic particle counters for liquids. Compatibility depends on the sensor, flow system and software, so we confirm by make and model before anything ships. Send us your model.

What's the difference between ISO 11171 and ISO 4406?

ISO 11171 is how a particle counter is calibrated, so that "4 µm(c)" means the same thing on every instrument. ISO 4406 is how fluid cleanliness is reported, as a three-number code built from counts at 4, 6 and 14 µm(c). You need the first for the second to mean anything.

How often should a particle counter be calibrated?

Many quality programs use an annual interval, plus recalibration after a sensor is repaired or replaced. Your own quality procedure or your customer's requirement is what governs, and we'll work to it.

How much does calibration cost?

It depends on the instrument, the calibration scope and how many units you send. Send the model and quantity and we'll reply with a written quote. There's no obligation.
