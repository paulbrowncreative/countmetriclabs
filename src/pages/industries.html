Industrial hydraulics & machinery

Protect the components that fail first

ISO 4406 · ISO 11500 · ISO 4021 · ISO 11171

Particles in the same size range as a component's internal clearances cause most wear in hydraulic systems. Servo and proportional valves, high-pressure piston pumps and precision actuators are usually the first to suffer, which is why equipment manufacturers set ISO 4406 cleanliness targets and warranty terms around them.

Where we help

  • Calibrating in-house lab counters so your trending data stays comparable year to year.
  • Independent particle counts on flushing and commissioning samples, new fluid deliveries and filter changes.
  • Investigating disagreements between your results and an OEM's or filter supplier's.

Aerospace & defense

Documentation that holds up to supplier audits

SAE AS4059 · NAS 1638 (legacy) · ISO 11171

Aerospace hydraulic and fuel system cleanliness is usually specified as an SAE AS4059 class, while older drawings and contracts may still reference NAS 1638. Either way, the result is only as credible as the calibration record behind the counter that produced it.

Where we help

  • ISO 11171 calibration with as-found and as-left data, so a supplier quality review can trace every result.
  • AS4059 reporting in cumulative or differential form, matched to the class format on your drawing.
  • NAS 1638 transitions: explaining what changes when a legacy spec moves to AS4059.

If your contract requires calibration from an accredited laboratory, tell us at the inquiry stage. We're not yet accredited and will say so plainly. Read our status.

Fuels & lubricants

An independent second opinion on cleanliness

ISO 4406 · ISO 11171

Blenders, distributors and end users increasingly check particle counts at handover points: after blending, in storage and at delivery. When a delivered fluid's code doesn't match the certificate it shipped with, an independent count settles the question faster than a debate.

Where we help

  • Particle counts on delivered or stored product, reported to the format in your supply agreement.
  • Calibration of lab counters used for quality control of fuels and lubricants within the scope of ISO 11171.
  • Method review for dark or additive-heavy fluids that challenge light-extinction counting.
